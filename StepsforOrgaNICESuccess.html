Steps for OrgaNICE Success

Step 1: Visit the Services page to explore how I can best support your organizing goals.

  • If you don’t see a service listed that you’re looking for, feel free to ask — as my mom always says, “It never hurts to ask.”
Services

Step 2: Book a Quick Complimentary Goals Session (Optional)

  • Schedule a brief, no-pressure call through my Calendly calendar.
  • Use this time to discuss your space, goals, and any challenges.
  • Explore the best approach tailored to your needs.
  • Get answers to any initial questions.
  • This consultation is optional but a great way to ensure we’re a perfect fit.
Schedule A Goals Session

Step 3: Book Your Organizing Session

  • Book your session anytime—no consultation required!
  • Sessions are scheduled in 4-, 6-, or 8-hour blocks via Calendly with real-time availability.
  • For larger or ongoing projects, we’ll plan your schedule together during the consultation or after your first session.
Book Your Session

Step 4: What to Expect During Your Session

  • Each session is hands-on and focused on creating real, visible progress in your space.
  • Sessions are tailored to your priorities and goals.
  • You choose how involved you’d like to be — some clients enjoy working side by side, while others prefer me to take the lead.
  • I’ll guide you through sorting, decision-making, and setting up practical systems that make sense for your lifestyle with any needed accommodations front of mind.
  • My goal is for you to feel supported, respected, and encouraged every step of the way.
  • By the end, you’ll not only see the difference in your space, but also feel a sense of relief and clarity moving forward.

Step 5: Payment

  • Payment is due at the end of each session.
  • Accepted payment methods: Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, Check, or Cash.
  • Need a receipt for cash or check payments? Just ask—I’m happy to provide one.

Step 6: Schedule Your Next Session

  • After your session, we’ll review what’s been accomplished and decide what kind of support you’d like next.
  • Easily use the same scheduling link to book your next appointment at a time that works best for your routine — I’m here to help you maintain a calm, functional space at your own pace.
  • Staying consistent goes a long way toward keeping your home organized and stress-free.
